5) Grammar: (not) as…as Comparisons

Equal comparison

Use as + adjective + as to say two things are the same.

Example: A nurse is as brave as a firefighter.

Not equal

Use not as + adjective + as to say one thing is less than another.

Example: Celebrities are not as helpful as volunteers.

Key points

The adjective stays in its base form (no -er, no more).

Works with any adjective: short or long.

Structure Example Meaning
as + adj + as My teacher is as patient as my mum. They are equally patient.
not as + adj + as Movie stars are not as brave as firefighters. Firefighters are braver.
as + adj + as (question) Is a doctor as important as a teacher? Asking if they are equal.

7) Guided Speaking: Modern Heroes Podcast

Option A: A real person

Describe a real person who is a modern hero.

  1. What do they do?
  2. What qualities do they have?
  3. Why are they a hero?

Option B: An imagined hero

Create an imagined modern hero.

  1. What is their name / job?
  2. What are their qualities and actions?
  3. How do they help people?

Option C: Argument

Who is more important?

  1. Visible heroes (celebrities) or invisible heroes (volunteers)?
  2. Give reasons using "as...as".
  3. What is your conclusion?
